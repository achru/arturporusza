# Surface brief: / (landing)

Mode: Read. The landing page is a quiet informational home — comprehension and wayfinding, zero selling pressure (persona: Feldenkrais not for sale, done from love).

Audience & job: Polish adults checking what Artur runs, when and where; movement teachers scouting his programs; returning visitors wanting terminy in seconds.

Action: follow links (event pages, Booksy, /o-mnie/, newsletter signup) presented as information, never as CTAs.

Direction (chosen 2026-07-30, decision page, seed 9ebfa753 + user steer "Figura but more minimalistic"): **Figura z jednej linii** — one continuous ink line draws a person mid-movement and is the site's entire graphic system (figure, rules, underlines). Paper white #fbfaf7, warm near-black ink, sanguine (red-chalk) accent for links/live markers only, single family Literata (opsz), typography carries all hierarchy. Photos render grayscale (ink world). One motion moment: the figure draws itself once on load; reduced-motion shows it drawn.

Memorable moment: the ruled line of the page rises into a reclining figure (klasyczna pozycja ATM) and returns to being a rule.

Cut by decision: stat grid, testimonial placeholders, gold CTA buttons, status chips, sticky mobile CTA, card grids, scroll-reveal animations, kalendarz filters. Oferta+Kalendarz merged into one chronological Terminy list.

Unresolved: MailerLite IDs, prices, legal fields remain empty by design; real testimonials may return later as quiet prose quotes.
